# Brand Identity

## Logo Concept

The logo plays the name straight: a dark junk bin marked `SHT` with a red arrow pushing the mess out. It reads quickly at small sizes, keeps the joke obvious, and still feels like a real service business.

## Assets

- `assets/logo-mark.svg` - compact mark for favicon, avatar, stickers, social profiles, and truck decals
- `assets/logo-horizontal.svg` - wider wordmark for invoices, flyers, cards, and larger web placements

## Visual Language

- Dark navy/black bin: reliable, work-ready, not flimsy
- Yellow `SHT`: construction/hauling energy
- Red arrow: action, removal, “get it out”
- Slight tilt: cheeky and memorable without looking sloppy

## Usage Notes

- Use the mark when space is tight.
- Use the horizontal logo when the full business name needs to be obvious.
- Keep the logo on light or plain backgrounds.
- Avoid placing it on busy photos unless it has a light background panel.
